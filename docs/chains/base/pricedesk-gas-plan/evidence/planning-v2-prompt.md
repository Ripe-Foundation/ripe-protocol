2. **Define the gas contract and its feasibility boundary.**

   A root pricing request is a PriceDesk operation outside a demonstrated budget-scoped callback from a source currently being evaluated. It includes protocol/user calls and source-originated standalone operations. `qualifyCallerPriceSource` must enforce its own root admission guarantee even when its caller is already registered.

   Root caller underfunding means the PriceDesk cannot supply a required source-call budget and preserve the proven continuation reserve because the enclosing caller supplied insufficient gas. Detect it before a reduced call where possible, and revert atomically rather than returning an ordinary zero/no-feed/no-op. This applies to non-strict entry points too. Very low gas may produce an ordinary transaction OOG rather than a custom error; it must not produce a successful misleading result.

   Parent-budget exhaustion is different: once an outer source receives its full configured allowance, its computation and nested dependency traversal must fit inside that allowance. A nested attempt may receive less than its nominal standalone ceiling without proving root caller underfunding. Failure within this fully supplied source envelope remains isolatable source failure. Allow healthy outer fallback when aggregate gas permits. Do not require another full global allowance at every nested frame, and do not promise a positive result for every genuinely failing source.

   This distinction is the semantic repair, not proof that a particular implementation can enforce it. The quote/conversion/qualification path is `@view`; source calls are static. No persistent or transient depth-counter writes, reentrancy-style write flags, or mutable shared budget accounting may be introduced on that path. STATICCALL restrictions propagate into descendants. Snapshot entry is stateful, but any static descendants retain their restrictions. See [EIP-214](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-214) and [EIP-1153](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1153).

   First build a bounded, local feasibility prototype. Registry membership is not proof of an active callback: registered sources also call PriceDesk during standalone validation, and a candidate can need callbacks before registration. A membership-based rule may be investigated, but it is not an approved solution or a loophole in the underfunding guarantee. Test registered standalone calls, true callbacks, unregistered candidate callbacks, re-qualification, removed/replaced sources, and adversarial registered sources. Force admission to the full effective quote budget regardless of membership.

   Prove that classification cannot silently weaken root behavior and that nested failure handling preserves source isolation. Trace failure propagation across non-reverting `raw_call`; source-provided revert bytes are not authoritative evidence of origin. Explicitly state which errors propagate and which remain source-local. If the root/nested contract cannot be proved inside the allowed scope, mark hardening blocked on a concrete design decision and give the smallest interface/caller change proposal with reproduction evidence. Do not ship an exemption under a different name.

   Ordinary correctly funded source reverts, malformed responses, and allowance exhaustion must preserve intended fallback. Preserve `_shouldRaise`/`mustRaiseOnZero` semantics for genuine source unavailability. Keep the retired `_staleTime != 0` behavior: strict rejection or non-strict zero is intentional.
