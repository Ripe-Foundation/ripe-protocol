## The main problems

**1. Configurability is sequenced last.**
- The prompt opens with "reproduce the failure" and pushes storage-backed budgets into Phase 5 "hardening".
- That phase runs only after five phases of measurement, has a four-hour cap, and stops at the first contradiction.
- The most likely outcome is a pile of evidence files and a NO-GO with no contract change.

- The prompt should say the real task plainly: governance-settable budgets with a per-source override.

**2. The "narrow" workstream is empty by the prompt's own rules.**
- Option 1, keeping the active desk, is declared not a qualified fix.
- Option 2, a config-only change, names only two levers, snapshot count and source order. Both are banned as pricing policy.

- I'd state that conclusion at the top and merge the two streams into one PriceDesk redeploy. The redeploy, with its 12-hour timelock and scale re-bootstrap, is the expensive part.

**3. Section B makes the root-versus-nested problem harder than it needs to be.**
- It sets this up as an open research problem and says neither default qualifies for production.
- I think per-source budgets plus a pre-call minimum-gas check (the Optimism `SafeCall` pattern), applied the same way in every frame, remove the dilemma.
- A starved inner desk reverts, and the outer `raw_call` contains that as an ordinary source failure, which the desk can isolate because it just proved it gave the source its full budget. No classifier or state writes are needed.
- Budgets then have to nest by dependency depth: Curve at least Undy, Undy at least Chainlink. The prompt already asks for that dependency graph.
- The prompt should warn off the post-call check (the OpenZeppelin forwarder style). A source that survives an inner out-of-gas and reverts with leftover gas slips past it.
- This is my reading of [PriceDesk.vy](../../../../contracts/registries/PriceDesk.vy) and the source callbacks, not something I tested. Make it approach one, and say it counts as production-qualified if the matrix passes.

**4. The envelope gate has no fault model.**
- "Required worst-case behavior" is never defined.
- If every source may burn its full budget on every quote, the per-source design allows 3 quotes per transaction, and even master's 250k design allows only 6.
